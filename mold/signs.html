Signs of Mold After a Leak

A musty, earthy smell is usually the first sign, often before anything is visible. Look for dark spots on drywall, baseboards and the back of carpet, paint that bubbles, and condensation on windows in a room that had water damage.

Mold behind walls often shows up as a smell near outlets or baseboards.

A moisture inspection shows where materials are still wet.

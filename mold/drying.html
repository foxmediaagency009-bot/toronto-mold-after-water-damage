How Proper Drying Prevents Mold

Drying aims to bring materials back to their normal moisture level, not just to make them feel dry. Readings are taken every day and compared with an unaffected area of the same home.

Air movers, dehumidifiers and, for floors and walls, targeted drying systems do the work. Anything that can't dry in time, like soaked insulation or carpet pad, is removed.

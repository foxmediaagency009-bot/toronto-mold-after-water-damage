Why Toronto Homes Get Mold

Humid Toronto summers keep basements damp, and many older homes have no vapour barrier on foundation walls. Finished basements trap moisture behind drywall and carpet.

Condos have their own risk: leaks between units that go unnoticed in wall cavities.

Basements that have flooded before are the most at risk.
